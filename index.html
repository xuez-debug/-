What is an internet meme?

An internet meme refers to a type of information that spreads rapidly among people via the internet. Anyone can use it to share their daily life, their views on certain matters, or anything else. They can consist of images, videos, GIFs, and more. As previously mentioned, anyone can create their own memes—including you.
