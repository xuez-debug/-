trollface

The Trollface image features a face with a mischievous smile; it was drawn by a student on September 19,2008, using Microsoft's drawing tool. It was originally used to mock clueless or obnoxious internet users. He is also one of the earliest and most famous members of the Rage Comic community.

/Image taken from Wikipedia.

He has also been described by La Tercera as the "Father of Memes." The Museo del Meme in Mexico City has even exhibited a bust of Trollface.
